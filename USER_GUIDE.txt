MailSift: clear the junk out of your Hotmail
============================================

Works with: Hotmail, Outlook.com, Live and MSN email addresses.

1. Double-click MailSift.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (It says that about any app that isn't from a big company.)

2. Your web browser opens on MailSift. Click "sign in with microsoft" and sign in
   with your email. Microsoft will ask if MailSift can access your mail. Click "Accept".

3. Choose how far back to look and click "run scan".

4. For each sender, pick keep, unsub, unsub+purge (also tidies away their old emails)
   or block. Or click "smart pick" to do the obvious ones for you.
   Tip: sort by "most per month" to find the noisiest senders.

   Tip: tap the star (☆) next to any sender you never want touched, like your bank.
   MailSift will leave them alone, even with "smart pick".

5. Click "review & apply", check the list, then "execute". Done.
   Changed your mind? "undo last clean-up" puts the emails back and unblocks.

6. Click "quit" (top right) when you're finished. If you just close the tab,
   MailSift switches itself off after about 10 minutes anyway.

Good to know
------------
- Only use a copy of MailSift.exe you got directly from Dean. Don't run one
  someone else has passed on.
- MailSift never sees your password. You sign in on Microsoft's own page.
- It runs on your computer only. Your emails don't go to anyone, including whoever
  gave you this app. It only reads the "envelope" of each email, never what's inside.
- Nothing is permanently deleted. Old emails go to Deleted Items or Junk, so you
  can get them back in Outlook.
- Senders marked "suspicious" can only be blocked. Unsubscribing from scammers just
  tells them your email address works.
- "forget me" on the scan screen signs you out and wipes everything MailSift has
  saved on your computer.
- To remove its access from your Microsoft account: account.live.com/consent/Manage
